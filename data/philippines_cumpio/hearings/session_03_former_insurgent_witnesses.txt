# Session H03 | Theme: testimony of witnesses described as former insurgents, and defense records in reply
# Roles: D2 = human rights defender (Defendant 2); FW = former-insurgent prosecution witness.

The prosecution called witnesses described as former insurgents. They gave accounts of cash handoffs and deliveries of food and materials.
At least two FWs are under government protection provided by the Philippine Army.
The defense argued that this protection gives the FWs strong incentives to testify for the prosecution.
One FW testified that D2 had ties with the Rural Missionaries of the Philippines as early as 2010.
The defense submitted D2's certificate of live birth and school records, showing she was 13 years old in 2010 and living in another province.
