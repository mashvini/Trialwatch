# Session H02 | Theme: evidence on the raid, prosecution and defense testimony
# Roles: D1 = journalist (Defendant 1); D2 = human rights defender (Defendant 2); PW = police or military prosecution witness.

PW testified that D1 and D2 were classified as highly dangerous, at risk level 5 on a scale of 1 to 5.
Several PWs said they knew little about the defendants' backgrounds.
A first entry team went in, frisked D1 and D2 on their beds, and declared the room clear.
PWs said light from the hallway partly lit the room; one said it was not pitch black.
Two entry-team officers went up to the beds to frisk the defendants; none of the entry team searched for weapons.
Asked about the weapons, one entry-team officer said he had not looked for them.
D1 and D2 were then taken out of the room. PWs said the decision to remove them had been made in advance by their ground commander.
One officer agreed there was no danger of harm once the defendants had been secured inside the room.
One officer put the time before the search team went in at six to eleven minutes.
A second search team then brought the defendants back to the room to carry out the warrant, which specifically authorized a search for weapons.
Search-team witnesses described the firearm and grenade as very obvious.
D1 testified that about 15 to 20 minutes passed between the first entry and the start of the search.
D1 testified that when she was brought back in, a firearm and a hand grenade were already on her bed.
D1 and D2 both deny that the firearm and the grenade were theirs.
D1 and D2 said the funds found in the room were part of a fundraiser for the Stand for Samar campaign.
