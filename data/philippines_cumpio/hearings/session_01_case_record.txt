# Session H01 | Theme: case record (arrest, charges, surveillance) | from case files reviewed by TrialWatch
# Roles: D1 = journalist (Defendant 1); D2 = human rights defender (Defendant 2); HRD-3 = another human rights defender.

D1 and D2 were arrested in a night raid on the room they rented in Tacloban City on 7 February 2020. Both have been held in detention since.
The first charges were illegal possession of firearms and explosives, based on a firearm and a hand grenade police and military say they found in the room.
About one year after the arrest, prosecutors added charges of terrorism financing under Republic Act No. 10168.
The terrorism-financing charges rest on former insurgents' accounts of cash handoffs and deliveries of food and materials, and on funds recovered from the room.
The prosecution relies on a 2017 presidential proclamation adopting a foreign terrorist designation of the CPP-NPA; the Anti-Terrorism Council's own designation is dated 9 December 2020.
An affidavit supporting the search warrant states that surveillance had been ongoing since October 2019.
The surveillance account relies on statements from an informant who is not identified.
On 6 February 2020, HRD-3 delivered a letter to the Commission on Human Rights saying they had information that a raid on their office was planned, and asking the Commission to certify they kept no illegal items. D1 had proofread the letter.
A Commission official confirmed the letter was received. The raid took place the next day, before any inspection.
